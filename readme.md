# 🎓 TribalScholar AI
### AI-Powered Scholarship Management System

## 📌 About the Project

**TribalScholar AI** is a web-based scholarship management system designed to help students, especially those from tribal communities, easily find and apply for educational scholarships.

The platform connects students and administrators in one place, making the scholarship application process simpler, faster, and more transparent.

Students can explore scholarships, check eligibility, submit applications, upload documents, and track their application status. Administrators can review applications, verify documents, and approve or reject scholarship requests.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Portal
- **Student Registration & Login** – Secure access to the platform.
- **Explore Scholarships** – View available scholarship opportunities.
- **Eligibility Checker** – Check whether students meet scholarship requirements.
- **Online Application** – Apply for scholarships through a simple form.
- **Document Upload** – Upload community certificates and supporting documents.
- **Application Tracking** – Track application status, including submitted, approved, or rejected.
- **AI Chatbot** – Get assistance with scholarship-related questions.
- **Announcements** – View scholarship updates and important information.

### 🛡️ Admin Portal
- **Admin Dashboard** – Manage scholarship applications from one place.
- **Application Review** – View student details and submitted applications.
- **Document Verification** – Access uploaded certificates for review.
- **Approve or Reject** – Update application decisions.
- **Scholarship Management** – Manage scholarship information.
- **Announcements Management** – Publish important updates for students.

## 🤖 AI Integration

TribalScholar AI includes an AI-powered assistant using **Google Gemini** to help students understand scholarship information, application procedures, eligibility requirements, and common scholarship-related questions.

## 🔄 How the System Works

1. Students register and log in to the platform.
2. They explore scholarships and check eligibility.
3. Eligible students fill out the application form.
4. Students upload the required certificates and documents.
5. Administrators review applications and verify documents.
6. Administrators approve or reject applications.
7. Students can track their application status through the dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of TribalScholar AI is to simplify scholarship management and improve access to educational financial assistance for tribal students.

The platform aims to reduce paperwork, improve transparency, save time, and make the scholarship application process easier for both students and administrators.
